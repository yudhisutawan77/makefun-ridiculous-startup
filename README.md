## The entry

> We certify houses as officially haunted: our mediums verify your ghost, and Airbnb hosts pay us because a certified haunting lets them charge 40% more a night.

**159 characters** (limit: 200, one sentence).

The pitch sounds like a joke — a startup that certifies ghosts — until the
second look: haunted stays already command a premium, hosts are the paying
customers, and a "certified haunting" is a trust mark they can price into
the nightly rate. Bizarre premise, real market, concrete buyer.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the single-sentence pitch to `/output/entry.txt` and
validates it against the bounty gate (UTF-8 text, 1–200 characters, not
only whitespace, exactly one sentence). It exits non-zero if the entry
is out of bounds.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
